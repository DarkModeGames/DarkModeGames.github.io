Recipes

Reusable patterns for building campaigns and plugins with Isometry data. Each recipe states a Problem, the Pattern that solves it, a complete YAML example, the entities it uses, and the gotchas to watch for. Every example validates on its own; sprites and the map's tiles are left for you to supply. For the fields of each entity, see All entity types.

Adding a recipe: drop a new web/recipes/<slug>.md file (the filename is the URL slug, the first # heading is the title), then add one bullet to the list below. Nothing else to wire up.

Actors & stats

  • Give every resource bounds, and hide internal state — min, max and reveal.
  • Set up each actor when it spawns — a spawn hook, because on_start is for players only.
  • Tag actors and filter area effects — make an area spell hit only enemies.
  • Derive stats from level and gear with Measures — always-current attack power.

Characters & progression

  • Let players pick their hero — start as a bodiless camera and choose a character.
  • A starter body that picks a class — walk around first, then choose; the choice sticks.
  • XP and level-up — a guarded Trigger chain that can't run away.
  • Talents unlocked at a level — a choice menu that puts a new skill on the hotbar.
  • Talent trees — points from level-ups, talents that unlock from the bottom up.
  • A character page with equipment slots — what you wear and your stats on one page.
  • Respawn a player on death — fall, a countdown, then rise at a graveyard.

Skills & class resources

  • Give a skill a cooldown — a countdown resource, a Timer, and a private "Not ready".
  • Skill tooltips with real numbers, and greyed-out skills — {{range:}}, {{dice:}}, ready and in_range.
  • Pace a skill: lock time and animation speed — match Action.time and Animation.rate.
  • Regenerate mana, decay rage — repeating Timers for class resources.
  • Energy and combo points — builders and finishers.
  • Health regeneration only out of combat — heal after a few quiet seconds.

Combat

  • Threat-based targeting and taunt — monsters attack the biggest threat; tanks pull them back.
  • Auto-attack: right-click an enemy, and fight back when hit — auto_interact, on_interact, reaction and start_auto_interact; also for peaceful repeats (tending a crop).
  • Monsters that come back — Deployment.respawn and walking home.
  • Stuns and interrupts with a swing lockout — stop a monster attacking for a while.
  • Slows that don't stack — temp_speed_target with a lock.
  • Shields and damage reduction with a hurt Trigger — wards and guard stances.

Monsters & bosses

  • Monster AI: acquire, chase, attack, leash — how a Strategy picks what to do each tick.
  • Pack call for help — idle packmates join the fight.
  • A summoner with a following legion — raise minions that march in formation and fight your fights.
  • Boss phases: a half-health line, adds and an enrage clock — a fight that changes as it goes.
  • Scale a boss to the party — health by the number of players present.
  • Leave a corpse for a few seconds — a death pose before the body goes.
  • A safe zone monsters won't enter — a ward ring at graveyards and arrivals.

Items & economy

  • Items as resources: equip, use, drop — inventory menus and one weapon at a time.
  • A vendor with a gold check — buy and sell from an NPC's menu.
  • Drop loot on death and let a player claim it — a bag that pays whoever walks onto it.

Quests & NPCs

  • Quest steps and an NPC that follows them — a step resource, menu options and kill credit.
  • Show a quest in the Quests page — Quest and Step over the same step resource.
  • Choices that change dialog and shops — sides, favour and branching dialog.
  • Ambient barks and failure feedback — public and private echo_self, with duration.

World & maps

  • Travel between maps through a gate — a Travel / Stay question on touch and on click.
  • Fast travel with waypoints — discoverable places with a travel menu.
  • Trees, walls and other tall art — untargetable actors over ghost obstacle tiles.
  • Help pathfinding round corners with Nav points — doorways and tight gaps.
  • Ranges, sight and area sizes — isometric pixels, everywhere.

Look & sound

  • Credit the people whose work you used — Main.credits, read from the System menu.
  • Floating combat numbers — combat_text on a Resource.
  • Recolour one sprite for many actors — Skins and Masks.
  • Parallax backgrounds and floor decals — scenery behind and under the map.
  • Sounds on animations and map ambience — one-shot and looping Sounds.
  • Screen effects and particles — rain, fog and glows (manual guide).
