Parallax backgrounds and floor decals

Problem. Beyond the edge of the map there is only flat colour, and you'd like a painted picture under the ground rather than only repeated tiles.

Pattern. A map takes two kinds of extra art:

  • background: a list of Parallax layers drawn behind the world. Each scrolls at effect times the camera's speed, so a low value (far mountains) moves slowly and gives depth.
  • floor: a list of Floor pictures drawn under the tiles, with their top-left corner at a Vector. They show through wherever the tile layers leave cells empty, so a painted backdrop can fill the gaps of a sparse tile layer.
Parallax:
  farHillsParallax:
    texture: assets/ground.png
    effect: 0.2
  nearMistParallax:
    texture: assets/ground.png
    effect: 0.5

Floor:
  townSquareFloor:
    location: squareCentreVector
    texture: assets/ground.png

Vector:
  townSpawnVector:
    x: 384
    y: 8
  squareCentreVector:
    x: 400
    y: 16

Map:
  townMap:
    name: Town
    tilemap: groundTilemap
    spawn: townSpawnVector
    background: [farHillsParallax, nearMistParallax]
    floor: [townSquareFloor]

Entities used. Floor, Map, Parallax, Vector

Gotchas.

  • List background layers from farthest to nearest, with effect rising as they come closer.
  • A Floor's location is its top-left corner, not its centre.
  • Floor pictures have no collision and don't affect where anyone can walk; the tiles decide that.
  • Parallax textures should tile or be large enough to fill the screen at your camera zoom.
  • For weather and screen-wide effects, see the effects guide.
