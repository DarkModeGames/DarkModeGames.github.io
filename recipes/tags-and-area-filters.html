Tag actors and filter area effects

Problem. Your fire nova hurts the caster, your friends and the scenery, not just the enemies.

Pattern. area_of_effect_* runs its sub-action on every actor whose centre is inside the radius: the caster, allies, enemies and decorations alike. Conditions can't test a Group, so give actors tag resources (isPlayerResource, isEnemyResource) set by a spawn hook, and start the sub-action with an if on the tag of @ (each actor hit).

Resource:
  healthResource:
    name: Health
    default: 100
    min: '0'
    max: '100'
  isEnemyResource:
    name: Is Enemy
    default: 0
    min: '0'
    max: '1'
    reveal: 999999

Condition:
  untaggedCondition:
    left: '$isEnemyResource'
    operator: '=='
    right: '0'
  targetIsEnemyCondition:
    left: '@isEnemyResource'
    operator: '=='
    right: '1'

Parameter:
  healthParameter:
    key: resource
    value: healthResource
  isEnemyParameter:
    key: resource
    value: isEnemyResource
  oneValueParameter:
    key: value
    value: '1'
  novaDamageParameter:
    key: expression
    value: '2d6+4'
  novaRadiusParameter:
    key: radius
    value: '100'
  novaHitParameter:
    key: action
    value: novaHitAction

Action:
  # The caster's skill: hit everything in 100 px...
  fireNovaAction:
    name: Fire Nova
    time: 1.0
    do: area_of_effect_at_self
    parameters: [novaRadiusParameter, novaHitParameter]
  # ...but only enemies take damage.
  novaHitAction:
    if: targetIsEnemyCondition
    do: minus_resource_target
    parameters: [healthParameter, novaDamageParameter]
  # Spawn hook for enemies (see the spawn-hook recipe).
  tagEnemyAction:
    if: untaggedCondition
    do: set_resource_self
    parameters: [isEnemyParameter, oneValueParameter]

Trigger:
  tagEnemyTrigger:
    resource: isEnemyResource
    action: tagEnemyAction

Skill:
  fireNovaSkill:
    name: Fire Nova
    description: Burns every enemy close around you.
    start: fireNovaAction

Actor:
  heroActor:
    name: Hero
    sprite: heroSprite
    skills: [fireNovaSkill]
  wolfActor:
    name: Grey Wolf
    sprite: wolfSprite
    triggers: [tagEnemyTrigger]

Entities used. Action, Actor, Condition, Parameter, Resource, Skill, Trigger

Gotchas.

  • Inside the sub-action, $ is the caster and @ is the actor being hit. Always filter on @.
  • The caster is inside its own area. An unfiltered "heal everyone" heals the caster too, which may be what you want.
  • targetable: false decorations are still hit. They hold every resource at its default, so tag 0 filters them out.
  • The tag Trigger above is the smallest possible hook: it fires on the first frame, and its guard lets it set the tag only once.
  • A radius of r reaches r px to the sides and r/2 px up or down the screen, between centres. See Distances and sizes.
