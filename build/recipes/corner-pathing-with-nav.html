Help pathfinding round corners with Nav points

Problem. Walkers take odd routes through a narrow doorway or a custom-shaped yard, or get stuck at a gap.

Pattern. Walkers plan paths across the map's walkable tiles and bend their path at corners. The corners of walls and obstacles are found automatically. Where that isn't enough (a doorway, a gap between props, a twisty path), add a Nav point: a Vector on a given map where walkers may also bend their path.

Vector:
  townSpawnVector:
    x: 384
    y: 8
  smithyDoorVector:
    x: 416
    y: 40
  yardGapVector:
    x: 352
    y: 72

Nav:
  smithyDoorNav:
    map: townMap
    location: smithyDoorVector
  yardGapNav:
    map: townMap
    location: yardGapVector

Entities used. Nav, Vector

Gotchas.

  • Place a Nav point in the middle of the opening, on walkable ground. A point off the walkable area or inside an obstacle is skipped with a warning when the map is built.
  • A point too tight for a large body is left out of that body's paths, so big monsters may still not fit.
  • Add Nav points only where you see a problem. Most maps need none.
  • Test with a walker that crosses the spot (a test actor with set_destination_self on a Timer) and watch it.
  • Big open fields next to roads can also cause long detours. Breaking them up with props helps.
