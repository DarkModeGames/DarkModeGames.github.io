Monsters that come back

Problem. A camp of wolves should fill up again some time after the players clear it, but not while a player is standing right there.

Pattern. Set respawn on each Deployment: seconds after its actor leaves the world for good (it dies and despawns) before a fresh one spawns at the same spot. The timer waits while a player is near the spot. A monster that wanders can be walked home with move_home_self, for example from a Strategy behaviour when the built-in distance_to_home measure grows too large.

Vector:
  wolfDenVector:
    x: 320
    y: 40

Deployment:
  denWolfDeployment:
    actor: wolfActor
    location: wolfDenVector
    respawn: 120

Condition:
  nearHomeCondition:
    left: '$distance_to_home'
    operator: '<'
    right: '200'

Action:
  goHomeAction:
    do: move_home_self

Behavior:
  stayNearHomeBehavior:
    action: goHomeAction
    goals: [nearHomeCondition]

Strategy:
  wolfStrategy:
    behaviors: [stayNearHomeBehavior]

Actor:
  wolfActor:
    name: Grey Wolf
    sprite: wolfSprite
    strategy: wolfStrategy

Map:
  townMap:
    name: Town
    tilemap: groundTilemap
    spawn: townSpawnVector
    deployments: [denWolfDeployment]

Entities used. Deployment, Strategy, Behavior, Condition, Action, Vector

Gotchas.

  • respawn: 0 (the default) means never: deployments spawn once, when the world is built.
  • A change of form (change_actor_*) is not "leaving for good", so it doesn't start the timer.
  • The respawned actor is fresh: every resource at its default, its spawn hook runs again (Spawn hook).
  • Respawn timers live on the host; they don't survive a server restart (the world rebuilds every deployment).
