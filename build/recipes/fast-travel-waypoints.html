Fast travel with waypoints

Problem. Once a player has found the shrine on the moor, they should be able to travel back to it from anywhere.

Pattern. A Waypoint marks a named place on a map. It appears on the player's map once discovered: the first time the player opens their map while within three quarters of their perception of it. Give the waypoint a menu; choosing the waypoint on the map opens it with the player as both caller and target, so a "Travel here" option uses *_self actions.

Resource:
  zoneResource:
    name: Zone
    default: 1
    min: '0'
    max: '99'
    reveal: 999999

Parameter:
  zoneParameter:
    key: resource
    value: zoneResource
  moorZoneValueParameter:
    key: value
    value: '2'
  moorMapParameter:
    key: map
    value: moorMap
  shrineLocationParameter:
    key: location
    value: shrineVector

Vector:
  townSpawnVector:
    x: 384
    y: 8
  shrineVector:
    x: 300
    y: 40

Action:
  travelToShrineAction:
    name: Travel here
    do: close_interaction
    then: travelToShrineZoneAction
  travelToShrineZoneAction:
    do: set_resource_self
    parameters: [zoneParameter, moorZoneValueParameter]
    then: travelToShrineMapAction
  travelToShrineMapAction:
    do: change_map_self
    parameters: [moorMapParameter, shrineLocationParameter]

Menu:
  shrineMenu:
    name: The Standing Shrine
    actions: [travelToShrineAction]

Waypoint:
  shrineWaypoint:
    name: The Standing Shrine
    description: Seven stones on the moor road.
    map: moorMap
    location: shrineVector
    menu: shrineMenu

Map:
  townMap:
    name: Town
    tilemap: groundTilemap
    spawn: townSpawnVector
  moorMap:
    name: The Moor
    tilemap: groundTilemap
    spawn: shrineVector

Entities used. Action, Map, Menu, Parameter, Resource, Vector, Waypoint

Gotchas.

  • Discovery is saved with the player, so a found waypoint stays found.
  • change_map_self to the map the player is already on does nothing. For a waypoint on the same map, use teleport_self_to_vector instead, or offer both options with an if on a zone resource.
  • The waypoint's location must be on walkable ground, since the player lands there.
  • A waypoint without a menu is only a marker.
  • To charge for travel, put an if on gold in front of the chain, as in A vendor with a gold check.
