Ranges, sight and area sizes

Problem. You set a melee range of 40 and an area of 100, and up-and-down the screen they seem half as big as side to side.

Pattern. Every ground distance is in isometric pixels: sqrt(dx² + (2·dy)²) between actor centres. A distance r reaches r px to either side and r/2 px up or down the screen, the same ellipse the ground is drawn on. Melee checks, spell ranges, leashes, sight and area radii all use it, so one number means the same thing everywhere. Write ranges as conditions on $distance_to_target.

Condition:
  inMeleeCondition:
    left: '$distance_to_target'
    operator: '<='
    right: '40'
  inSpellRangeCondition:
    left: '$distance_to_target'
    operator: '<='
    right: '260'
  withinLeashCondition:
    left: '$distance_to_target'
    operator: '<='
    right: '360'

Parameter:
  quakeRadiusParameter:
    key: radius
    value: '120'
  quakeEachParameter:
    key: action
    value: quakeEachAction

Action:
  quakeAction:
    do: area_of_effect_at_self
    parameters: [quakeRadiusParameter, quakeEachParameter]
  quakeEachAction:
    do: wait

Actor:
  heroActor:
    name: Hero
    sprite: heroSprite
    base: 16
    perception: 240
    salience: 240

Entities used. Action, Actor, Condition, Parameter

Gotchas.

  • perception is how far an actor sees, salience how far away it can be seen. One actor sees another when the ellipses overlap, so a monster with perception 300 notices a player with salience 240 at about 540 px.
  • Keep a monster's sight wider than its leash, or it loses its target at the edge of sight before the leash check runs.
  • An area of radius r hits actors whose centre is within r. Big actors don't reach further.
  • $distance_to_target to an actor on another map is huge, so range checks fail safely.
  • Vector positions and sprite sizes are plain pixels, not ground distances. See Distances and sizes for the full table.
