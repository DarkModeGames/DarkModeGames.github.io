Floating combat numbers

Problem. You want damage and healing to float up as numbers over the actor that took them, without writing an echo into every attack.

Pattern. Turn on combat_text on the Resource. Every change to it then floats up as a number over the actor, in gain_color when it rises and loss_color when it falls. Players see it on their own character, on actors they hurt or healed, and on actors they are targeting.

Resource:
  healthResource:
    name: Health
    default: 100
    min: '0'
    max: '100'
    combat_text: true
    gain_color: '#5ec96a'
    loss_color: '#d04040'
  manaResource:
    name: Mana
    default: 50
    min: '0'
    max: '50'
    combat_text: true
    gain_color: '#5aa0e0'

Entities used. Resource

Gotchas.

  • It is off by default. Turn it on only for numbers players care about. Mana ticking up every two seconds can be noisy.
  • The colours are optional (green for gains and red for losses by default). They take a hex code or a colour name.
  • A refill at a respawn floats too ("+100"). That is usually fine.
  • For words rather than numbers, use echo_self.
