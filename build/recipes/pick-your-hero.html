Let players pick their hero

Problem. You want each player to choose a character at the start instead of always controlling the same one.

Pattern. Leave Main's actor unset. When a campaign names no starting actor, each player begins as a null actor — a perfect "character select" vantage point. Since the null actor can't walk up and touch anything, give each hero a menu instead of an on_touch action: right-clicking a hero opens its menu from any distance, and the menu's action turns the player into that hero (change_actor_self).

# No `actor` on Main -> players start as the null actor.
Main:
  main:
    map: townSquare

# Heroes standing in the world, each ready to be taken over.
Actor:
  knight:
    name: Knight
    sprite: knightSprite
    menu: knightMenu
  mage:
    name: Mage
    sprite: mageSprite
    menu: mageMenu

Menu:
  knightMenu:
    name: Actions
    actions:
    - becomeKnight
  mageMenu:
    name: Actions
    actions:
    - becomeMage

Action:
  becomeKnight:
    do: change_actor_self
    parameters: [asKnight]
  becomeMage:
    do: change_actor_self
    parameters: [asMage]

Parameter:
  asKnight:
    key: actor
    value: knight
  asMage:
    key: actor
    value: mage

# Stand the heroes in the square, near where players start.
Map:
  townSquare:
    name: Town Square
    tilemap: groundTilemap
    spawn: squareSpawn
    deployments: [knightStand, mageStand]

Vector:
  squareSpawn:
    x: 384
    y: 8
  knightSpot:
    x: 344
    y: 8
  mageSpot:
    x: 424
    y: 8

Deployment:
  knightStand:
    actor: knight
    location: knightSpot
  mageStand:
    actor: mage
    location: mageSpot

groundTilemap stands for your own TileMap. Right-click a hero to open its menu and select the action. See the action reference for change_actor_self, and Campaign structure for Main.

Entities used. Action, Actor, Deployment, Main, Map, Menu, Parameter, Vector

Gotchas.

  • The null actor has unlimited reach: its right-click opens a hero's menu from anywhere. A real character would walk up and the menu opens once they are close.
  • The null actor holds no resources. After the change, the new hero starts with every resource at its default; set anything else up with a spawn hook on the hero.
  • The deployed heroes stay where they stand. A player who picks the knight becomes a second knight, and the standing knight remains for the next player.
  • The choice is saved with the player, so they come back as the hero they picked. To stop a player choosing twice, check a resource in the menu action's if.
