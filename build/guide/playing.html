Playing Isometry

Controls

These are the default keybinds. Every binding can be remapped (keyboard/mouse and gamepad) from the options menu.

Movement

  • Right Click — set a destination; your character paths to it around obstacles.
  • W / A / S / D — move directly in a direction (hold to keep moving).
  • Arrow Keys — change your facing direction (bearing).
  • N — switch between running and walking. Walking is half pace.
  • Flag button (left-hand HUD buttons, above the eye) — pick up a flag, then left-click to place it. On open ground your character walks to the flag. On a character, yours follows them, and talks to them or opens their menu on arrival. Right-click or press the button again to put the flag away.

Camera

  • L — toggle camera lock on your character.
  • Space — recenter the camera on your character (hold to keep it centered).
  • = — zoom in.
  • - — zoom out.

Character

  • U — open the Character page: what you wear in each slot, and your stats. Pick a slot to see the items you carry that fit it, and pick one of those to equip or unequip it.

Quests

  • J — open the Quests page: your quests, what to do now, and the steps already done. Tick Show completed to see finished quests too.

Targeting

  • Alt (hold) — outline everything you can target nearby, like Diablo's Alt. On a gamepad: the Share / capture button (rebind it in the gamepad menu).
  • Tab — cycle to the next target (nearest first, then each one in view in turn).
  • ` (Grave) — move to the current target.
  • V — follow the current target. Your character keeps walking after it and waits when close. Press V again, move, or change target to stop.
  • E — select the nearest target.
  • Q — cycle to the previous target.
  • C — cycle to the next target group.
  • Z — cycle to the previous target group.
  • X — target yourself.
  • Esc — clear the current target.
  • Click the icon on a plate — interact with that character, as Enter does with a target: you walk up to talk, trade, loot or fight. Works on the target plate and on focus plates.
  • Show Outlines button (bottom of the left-hand HUD buttons, an eye) — hold it down with the mouse to outline everything you can target, as holding Alt does.

Focus

A focus plate keeps a character's plate in a corner of the screen, so you can watch a friend's health or a boss's while you target something else. Each corner holds one.

  • R / T / F / G — the four corners (top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right). Tap with a target to pin it there. Tap again later to target the pinned character. Hold to clear the corner.
  • Pin icons — each corner shows a small pin. Hover it for a reminder. Click it to pin your target there (replacing whoever was there); with no target, a click targets the pinned character. Right-click clears it. The pins hide with the other HUD buttons (Show Menu Buttons in the options menu).
  • H — clear every focus corner and start fresh.
  • A pinned character who leaves the world (dies and despawns, or goes where you can't see them) leaves its corner by itself after a couple of seconds.

Skills

  • 1 – 9 — activate the skills in hotbar slots 1 through 9.
  • Skills may require a target to be selected.
  • Some skills are instant; others have casting animations.
  • Hold a skill key to charge it (if the skill supports charging).
  • Mouse-over casting. With the mouse over a character, a skill key casts on that character instead of your target, and your target stays selected: heal a friend under the cursor while keeping the enemy targeted. With the mouse over nothing, the skill goes to your target as usual. Clicking a hotbar button always uses your target (ctrl+click casts on yourself). Turn it off with Mouse-over Casting in the options menu (the default is ON).

Menus & Views

  • Enter — open the selection menu for the current target.
  • Backspace — open the global menu.
  • M — toggle the map view.
  • O — toggle the resources view.
  • Q / E (left / right shoulder on a gamepad) — step through the category chips over the list.
  • / (Y on a gamepad) — search by name. Esc clears the search; a second Esc closes the view.
  • Page Up / Page Down (the triggers) — turn the page.
  • K — toggle the skills view. Pick a skill up (click or accept) and put it on another key to swap them.
  • P — toggle the talents view: your talent trees, read from the bottom up. Click a talent to put a point in.
  • Minions (only while you have some): a strip under the target plate lists them with their orders. Click chips to pick some (none = all), then Attack, Follow or Stay. F1 / F2 / F3 give the orders, F4 picks the next minion, I hides or shows the strip. On a gamepad, Back enters command mode: D-pad left/right pick, X / A / Y give Attack / Follow / Stay; Back or B leaves it.
  • Esc — close the current menu.

Typing with a gamepad

Chat and the resources search can be typed into with a gamepad alone. When one of them takes the cursor and your last press was on a gamepad, an on-screen keyboard opens at the bottom of the screen (at the top if the text box is low). It never opens for keyboard or mouse players.

  • D-pad / left stick — move between keys; rows wrap round at the edges.
  • A — press the selected key.
  • B — delete the letter before the cursor; on an empty box, cancel.
  • X — space.
  • Y — shift, for one capital letter. The Caps key keeps capitals on.
  • Start — done: chat sends the line, the search returns to the list.
  • Left / right shoulder — move the cursor.
  • Cancel puts the text back the way it was. In chat it also closes the chat.

The keys can also be clicked. To turn the keyboard off, set On-Screen Keyboard to OFF in the options menu (the default is AUTO).

Gameplay Mechanics

Resources

Resources are numeric values tracked on your character (health, mana, gold, and so on). They display on your data plate and can be viewed by right-clicking your resource bar.

  • Resources have minimum and maximum values.
  • Resources can regenerate via timers.
  • Resources can trigger actions when they change (for example, death at 0 health).

Skills & Actions

Skills are bound to the number keys 1–9 by default. Pressing a skill key executes its associated action, which may:

  • Deal damage to a target
  • Heal yourself
  • Move to a location
  • Display information
  • Trigger conditional logic

Perception & Vision

Characters have a perception radius. You can only see actors within your perception range. Actors also have a visibility setting (salience) that affects how easily you can spot them.

Groups & Factions

Actors belong to groups (players, enemies, neutral). Group membership affects targeting and determines the colored outline on characters.

Multiplayer

Hosting a Game

  1. Launch with --network=host (or use your campaign's own launcher, if it has one).
  2. Share your IP address and port with players.
  3. Players connect with --network=client pointing at your address.

For details, see Hosting & Networking.

Joining a Game

  1. Get the server's IP and port from the host.
  2. Launch with --network=client --uri=SERVER_IP --port=PORT.
  3. Enter the same campaign name along with your credentials.

Authentication

Isometry keeps your password secure and encrypted. Players sign in with their username and password to join and spawn.

Troubleshooting

Campaign won't load

  • Verify the .zip file exists in your campaign directory.
  • Check the campaign name matches (case-sensitive, no .zip extension).
  • Run with --log-level=trace for detailed error output.
  • Validate the campaign directly with --validate=/path/to/campaign.zip.

Can't connect to server

  • Verify the server is running and the port is open.
  • Check firewall settings (the UDP port must be reachable).
  • Ensure both client and server use the same campaign version.

Performance issues

  • Reduce the zoom level.
  • Play on maps with fewer simultaneously visible actors.
  • Check --log-level=trace for bottleneck information.
